# How to be a software dev in the AI landscape

- **Learn fundamentals before relying on AI** — you need to know what good looks like to evaluate what AI gives you
- **Study failure post-mortems** — read outage reports from Cloudflare, AWS, Google etc.; understanding how systems fall apart builds intuition documentation can't
- **Struggle before reaching for AI** — read the stack trace, trace through code, form a hypothesis first; use AI after the attempt, not instead of it
- **Never ship code you can't explain** — be able to defend every line: why this library, why this pattern, what are the trade-offs
- **Prompt for the why, not just the answer** — ask AI for multiple approaches with pros/cons; you learn more and often get a better answer
- **Find pockets for deliberate learning** — downtime, side projects, unpopular tickets; be intentional about when you're building real skills vs just shipping
- **Use AI as a tutor, not a doer** — ask it to explain things at depth, not just solve problems for you
- **Your value is judgment, not output** — the ability to look at code and know whether it's good is what companies will pay for going forward
