## How to Not Be "The Artist Who Became a Manager"

**On genuine curiosity vs. going through the motions:**
- [ ] Stop performing management rituals you don't believe in; do fewer things with more sincerity

**On self-awareness:**
- [ ] Acknowledge the gap between what you're excellent at (the craft) and what managing requires (the people)
- [ ] If someone gives you a detailed, honest answer to a casual question — recognize that as a gift, not a threat

**On preparation:**
- [ ] Have a point of view — "what do you think?" only works if you're willing to share what *you* think too
- [ ] After a 1:1, write one thing down that you'll follow up on next time

**The hard one:**
- [ ] If your output is your art and your identity — be honest about whether you're willing to let someone else's growth matter as much to you as your own work. If not, stay an individual contributor and own that choice.
